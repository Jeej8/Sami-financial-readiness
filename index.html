03 · What are banks & fintechs trying to achieve?

The ecosystem has
a two-sided problem.

THE SME SIDE

“Help me understand my position.”

A business owner needs to know what their numbers say, what could weaken a financing application, and what they can improve before asking for funding.

THE FINANCIAL SIDE

“Give me usable, trustworthy information.”

Financial institutions need reliable financial information and efficient processes to evaluate customers and deliver better digital services.

THE GAP

Between the two is a preparation layer.

Saudi Arabia is improving access, funding channels and financial data infrastructure. Our question became: what if we build the layer that helps an SME understand and improve its financial readiness before entering the financing journey?

07 · The idea

Meet سمي.

A digital financial-readiness check for SMEs — turning financial data into clarity, explanations and next actions.

First, what is an “engine”?

An engine is simply the part of a system that takes inputs, processes them, and produces a useful output. Think of a car engine: fuel goes in → the engine processes it → movement comes out.

INPUTBusiness datarevenue · expenses · debt · cash flow
→
ENGINEFinancial analysiscalculates indicators + explains patterns
→
OUTPUTReadiness insightstrengths · risks · next actions
01 · CONNECT

Bring in financial data

Open Banking with consent, or demo/synthetic data for the MVP.

02 · UNDERSTAND

Analyze the business

Revenue, expenses, cash flow, debt and financial patterns.

03 · ASSESS

Show readiness

A transparent readiness assessment — not a loan approval.

04 · EXPLAIN

Tell me why

AI explains what is helping or hurting the assessment.

05 · ACT

Give next steps

Prioritized actions to strengthen the business before seeking financing.

Important boundary: سمي does not approve, reject or guarantee financing. The financial institution remains responsible for the final financing decision.
08 · What the SME sees

Not a spreadsheet.
A conversation.

Business readiness

READY TO IMPROVE
Strength · stable revenueStrength · payment patternImprove · short-term debtImprove · cash-flow volatility
ASK سمي
Why is my readiness 74?
Your strongest signals are stable revenue and healthy payment patterns. The main factors lowering the assessment are short-term debt and cash-flow volatility.
What should I improve first?
Start with cash-flow stability. Then reduce short-term debt where possible. Improving these areas could make your financial profile more consistent.
WHY AI BELONGS HERE

AI is the interpreter — not the decision-maker.

The calculations should be deterministic and explainable. GenAI turns those results into plain-language explanations, prioritizes actions and answers questions about the report.

No “AI predicts your approval.” No black-box promise. No guarantee.
09 · Why now technically?

The infrastructure
is catching up.

In March 2026, SAMA began licensing fintech companies to provide Open Banking services after the regulatory sandbox phase.

OPEN BANKING

Less manual entry. More usable data.

With customer consent, financial information can be securely shared with supervised entities. That creates a foundation for products that use real financial data instead of asking SMEs to reconstruct their finances manually.

MVP REALITY

We do not need a bank API tomorrow.

For the hackathon demo, we can use synthetic data or a mocked connection — while designing the architecture around the real Open Banking model.

SAMA says Open Banking is designed to enable customers to securely share financial information with supervised entities, with customer consent and privacy protections.

11 · Credibility & boundaries

Useful AI.
Responsible AI.

WE DO

Explain financial readiness

Use deterministic calculations for core indicators and GenAI to translate those results into understandable explanations and actions.

WE DO NOT

Approve or reject financing

سمي does not replace a bank's credit assessment, make lending decisions, or guarantee that an SME will receive financing.

DATA

Consent first

Open Banking data would only be used through authorized, consent-based connections. For the hackathon, synthetic/demo data keeps the prototype safe.

The credibility test: every number we calculate should be explainable, every AI recommendation should trace back to an observable factor, and the final financing decision stays with the financial institution.
