# سمي | Sami

### SME Financial Readiness Engine

> سمي، وش بغيتي؟

**Know where you stand. Know what to do next.**

سمي (Sami) is an AI-powered financial readiness platform designed to help Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) understand their financial position before seeking financing.

Instead of going directly from:

"I need financing" → "Let me apply and see what happens"

سمي creates a preparation step in between.

It analyzes business financial information, explains the factors affecting financial readiness, and provides actionable recommendations to help business owners understand what they can improve.

The experience starts with a simple question:

> **سمي، وش بغيتي؟**

From there, the conversation between the SME and the AI begins.
